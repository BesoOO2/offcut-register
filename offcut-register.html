Sync

Your register is saved on this device. Sync keeps a copy in a secret GitHub gist so your PC and phone match. Set it up once on each device with the same token.

  1. On GitHub, go to Settings › Developer settings › Personal access tokens › Tokens (classic).
  2. Generate a new classic token. Tick only gist and set expiration to No expiration.
  3. Copy the token, paste it below and press Connect.

How IDs work

PIN-19-023Planks and sheets under 30 mm thick use the thickness.
PIN-90-021Beams 30 mm or thicker use the height (90 of a 90×45).

The last part is the piece number. It is unique across all materials, so typing 21 in Find goes straight to that piece. Cutting a piece keeps its ID. Ripping makes new pieces with new numbers and marks the original used.

Import

Columns needed: material, length, width, thickness. Optional: treatment, finish, location, project, notes, status, added. Rows without a seq get new numbers. Rows with a seq replace that piece. You can paste straight from a spreadsheet.
